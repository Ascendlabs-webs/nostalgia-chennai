Before you write in

Orders
Are your collectibles authentic?
Yes — every figure and statue is sourced through official or verified channels and checked before it's listed. Ask for closer inspection photos any time.
How do pre-orders work?
A deposit secures your unit. We track manufacturer timelines (which can shift) and collect the balance once stock arrives in Chennai.
Do you ship outside Chennai?
Yes — across India, boxed and padded so nothing shifts in transit.
Selling collections
Can I sell a single figure, or only full collections?
Either. We quote single pieces as readily as full collections.
How fast is payment when I sell?
Walk-ins are paid on the spot after inspection. Mailed-in pieces are quoted from photos first, then paid the day they arrive and pass inspection.
What do payments depend on?
