A collection that outgrew its shelves.

Nostalgia Chennai began as a personal collection — the figure saved up for, the statue once only seen in magazines, the anime line that never officially shipped to India. Now it's a boutique for the same obsession.

Collector-first, always.

Every piece is checked for condition before it's listed, boxed the way a collector would want it boxed, and priced against what the piece is actually worth right now.

These are not just toys — pieces of childhood, fandom and identity.
