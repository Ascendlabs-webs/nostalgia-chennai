QUESTIONS

Before you write in

Are your collectibles authentic?+
Yes — every figure and statue is sourced through official or verified channels and checked before it's listed. If you'd like closer inspection photos before you buy, just ask.
How do pre-orders work?+
Pre-orders require a deposit to secure your unit. We keep you updated on manufacturer timelines, which can shift, and collect the balance once stock arrives in Chennai.
Do you ship outside Chennai?+
Yes — collectibles are shipped across India, boxed and padded so nothing shifts or gets crushed in transit.
Can I sell a single figure, or only full collections?+
Either. We quote single pieces as readily as full collections — condition and demand set the price either way.
How fast is payment when I sell?+
Walk-ins are paid on the spot after inspection. Mailed-in pieces are quoted from photos first, then paid the day they arrive and pass inspection.
